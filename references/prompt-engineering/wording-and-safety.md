# Meaning-preserving wording policy

QHSJ may normalize casual, ambiguous or technically noisy wording into precise production language. Examples include turning a vague visual adjective into observable geometry, material, lighting or camera properties.

Do not maintain a library whose purpose is to disguise prohibited requests, defeat moderation, or bypass access controls. A textual change is permitted when it improves clarity and preserves an otherwise allowed intent; it is not permitted when its purpose is evasion.

When a platform rejects allowed content because of an ambiguous phrase, prefer a neutral professional description of the same observable property and record the transformation. When the underlying content itself is disallowed, use `safe_reframe` and preserve only a permitted creative objective.
