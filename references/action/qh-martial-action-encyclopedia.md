## 1. 现代格斗 / 拳脚动作词典（AI 剧直接套用）

> 原则：站位、拳法、腿法、防守、步法、收势写清楚，角色动作才有节奏、有重量，不容易乱。

### 1.1 拳法
| 招式 | 英文 | 动作描述 |
|---|---|---|
| **前手直拳** | Jab | 前手快速直线刺出，探距、干扰、试探 |
| **后手直拳** | Cross | 后手蹬地转腰发力直线重击，主力拳 |
| **勾拳** | Hook | 手臂弯成弧形，从侧面横向击打下颌/肋骨 |
| **上勾拳** | Uppercut | 从下向上垂直勾击下颌，近身爆发 |
| **肘击** | Elbow Strike | 屈肘用肘尖横向/纵向击打 |

### 1.2 腿法
| 招式 | 英文 | 动作描述 |
|---|---|---|
| **前踢** | Front Kick | 提膝小腿前弹，击打膝/腹 |
| **横扫踢** | Roundhouse Kick | 转胯拧腰，小腿横向扫击肋/头 |
| **侧踢** | Side Kick | 侧身支撑，另一腿直线侧推击 |
| **后旋踢** | Spinning Kick | 转身 360° 借旋转力甩出踢击 |
| **膝撞** | Knee Strike | 提膝撞击大腿/腹/头，近身缠抱时用 |

### 1.3 防守与闪避
| 招式 | 英文 | 动作描述 |
|---|---|---|
| **格挡** | Block | 用前臂或手掌直接阻挡来势 |
| **拍挡** | Parry | 用手掌或前臂将来拳拍开，改变路线 |
| **外闪** | Slip | 头部与上体向外侧闪开攻击线 |
| **下潜** | Duck / Drop | 屈膝下蹲从攻击下方钻过，接反击 |
| **摇闪** | Roll | 头部画圆摇避，接勾拳反击 |
| **后撤步** | Pivot / Step Back | 脚步后撤或转脚，拉开距离或变换角度 |

### 1.4 步法
```
前滑步 / 后滑步 / 侧滑步 / 绕环步（Circling）/
冲步（突进）/ 撤步（撤同时反击）/ 垫步（Hop）
```

### 1.5 收势
每段打斗必须有收势，否则动作"飘"：
```
收拳护腮 / 落步转身 / 定式站桩 / 甩腕松力 / 衣摆余波微动
```

## 2. 少林拳典型招式（中式武侠风）

> 出自 DB4101/T 74—2023《少林拳套路技术规范》，可用于古风武侠/国术片。

| 招式 | 动作要点 |
|---|---|
| **并步冲拳** | 震脚与冲拳同时完成，屈蹲腿接近水平，力达拳面 |
| **跨虎势（跨虎登山）** | 左手屈臂经右肩向下按压，右手屈臂上提至太阳穴处；左虎爪手下按至左腿膝关节外侧，右腿撤步屈膝下蹲，左脚尖点地，目视前方 |
| **虎爪** | 五指张开屈曲如虎爪，下按/上扑，力达爪指 |
| **震脚** | 落脚重震地面，配合发声与气势 |

**风格关键词**：刚劲有力、虎虎生风、拳出入如箭、收回如抽丝、发声助威（"嚯""哈"）。

## 3. 器械套路（棍术 / 刀术）

### 3.1 棍术典型动作（国际武联）
- **斜上平抡**：身体右转 360°，左脚向前扣步，右脚撤步，两腿微屈；左手离棍随转体向左上伸出；右手握棍，使棍梢随转体经左向右在头部斜上方平抡一周，目视斜后方。
- **枪扎一条线，棍打一大片**：棍法强调横扫、抡劈、戳挑、架格。

### 3.2 刀术典型动作（国际武术竞赛套路）
- **缠头裹脑**：刀贴身绕头/绕背，护头颈。
- **跳步劈刀**：右脚蹬地起跳，身体向左后拧转，左腿向左后上方屈膝抬起；右手持刀经右向前、向左摆至左腋下；左臂随体转摆动。
- **刀随身走，步到刀到**：刀术要快、猛、疾，配合跳跃翻转。

## 5. AI 动作戏写作纪律（来自一线创作者）

### 5.1 站位写法
- 不要写"两人打起来了"，要写：
  ```
  两人相距三步对峙，主角左脚在前虚点，右脚在后重心下沉，
  对手右手前探护头，左手垂在腰侧伺机反击。
  ```

### 5.2 动作链写法（攻防回合）
```
0-2s：主角前手Jab刺探对手面门，对手外闪Slip躲开；
2-4s：主角后手Cross重击，对手拍挡Parry格开顺势贴身；
4-6s：对手膝撞Knee撞向主角大腿，主角下潜Duck钻过同时上勾拳Uppercut击中对手下颌；
6-8s：对手后退两步，主角垫步Hop突进横扫踢Roundhouse踢向对手肋部；
8-10s：对手格挡Block，主角落步收势收拳护腮。
```

### 5.3 重量感来源
- 每拳每脚都要写**攻防反应**：打中的一方要有"被击飞/后退/护痛"的反馈，否则像打空气。
- 落地要有**震感**：蹬地、震脚、地面龟裂、灰尘扬起。
- 服装要有**物理反馈**：衣摆被拳风带起、汗甩飞、发丝飘散。

### 5.4 避免的错误
- ❌ 只写形容词："凶狠地打斗""激烈的搏斗"。
- ❌ 一次塞超过 3 个动作在 2 秒内，AI 会乱。
- ❌ 不写收势，动作像断片。
- ✅ 用动作演情绪："锦衣卫率先快步冲锋抽出剑向前突袭，刺客灵巧地闪避、顺势挥剑反击"——不要写"凶狠""冷静"。

## 一、动作戏 Prompt 的核心原则

Scenic 指出：写 `a fight scene` 模型只会给你一个静态宽镜头；写 `steadicam whip-pan tracking the impact, low-angle fast push-in as the fighter launches` 才像导演在调度。**动作 prompt 必须把三层拆开写：身体（body）+ 相机（camera）+ 能量弧线（energy arc）**。

ImagePromptGallery 总结的"写实决斗"质量锚点：
> `ARRI Alexa Mini LF, anamorphic lens, professional action cinematography, dynamic camera language, layered composition, deep composition, high-speed action film pacing, cinematic motion blur, dramatic volumetric lighting, dust, debris, realistic body mechanics. No comedy, no floating, no anime physics, every hit carries weight and momentum.`

关键否定词（Negative Prompt）：`superpowers, glowing weapon, energy slash, magic particles, aura, lightning, impossible acrobatics, floating body, goofy posing, extra limbs, distorted hands, unreadable choreography, excessive motion blur`。

## 二、时间码分镜结构（最有效的动作 prompt 范式）

从盲僧、Oldboy 巷战、双节棍三个例子提炼出的标准结构：

```
[CINEMATIC SETUP] 类型/影调/调色/胶片
[CHARACTERS] 谁、穿什么、拿什么、体态
[LOCATION] 在哪、天气、环境细节
[TIMELINE]
  [0:00–3] 镜头编号：景别+机位+动作+情绪
  [3–6] ...
[SFX] 音效
[MUSIC] 音乐
[QUALITY ANCHOR] 质量约束
```

### 范例 A：盲僧以一敌多（MyStage，12 秒）
- `(0-2s) wide establishing shot, monk standing still in center, wind moving fabric, creatures circling, tension builds`
- `(2-4s) slow push-in close-up on monk's face, eyes closed, subtle head tilt sensing movement`
- `(4-6s) sudden attack, monk reacts instantly, precise sidestep + redirection, fluid motion`
- `(6-8s) chained combat sequence, spinning strikes, controlled movements, each impact sending creatures flying backward with stylized motion`
- `(8-10s) slow motion highlight: mid-air dodge + counter, cloth movement and body rotation emphasized, creatures suspended briefly before being thrown away`
- `(10-12s) final burst of speed, monk flows through remaining opponents in one continuous movement, camera orbiting rapidly`

**镜头语言组合**：`mix of slow dolly-ins + orbit moves + whip pans, transitions masked by body motion and impacts, alternating real-time and slow motion, continuous fluid sequence`。

### 范例 B：Oldboy 一镜到底巷战（15 秒，横向侧移长镜头）
- `[CINEMATIC SETUP] Gritty neo-noir, single continuous side-scrolling long take, raw unglamorous choreography, exhausted realistic combat, desaturated teal-green grade, harsh fluorescent and sodium vapor lighting, 35mm film grain.`
- `[0:00–3] Static wide profile — The Man stands alone at left end, hammer low, breathing heavy. Thugs fill the right, slowly closing in.`
- `[3–6] Lateral tracking right — The Man walks into the group swinging hammer in wide brutal arcs, two drop, rest swarm from both sides.`
- `[6–9] Lateral tracking continues — takes hits, staggers against brick wall, headbutts one, elbow into another, movements slower and heavier.`
- `[9–12] Exhausted brawling in steam, drags a thug down, rises using the wall, swings hammer one final time.`
- `[12–15] Slow push-in — man stands alone among fallen, swaying, hammer dripping, as fluorescent light flickers.`
- `[MUSIC] Melancholic waltz strings over the violence, ironic and elegant counterpoint.`

### 范例 C：双节棍个人秀（6 镜切分，每镜明确焦距/动作/SFX）
- Shot 1: `Hard cut into action. Extreme close-up, 35mm, nunchaku already spinning fast inches from camera, aggressive motion blur, focused eyes visible behind.`
- Shot 2: `Match cut to medium-wide low angle, 28mm, smooth side track. Pivots hard, knee bent, torso rotating, wrist rolls into side passes and underarm transitions.`
- Shot 3: `Tight hand-control close-up, 50mm, controlled handheld push-in. Wrist wraps, fingers, chain, wood texture. Aggressive wrist rolls, clean underarm pass, sharp recovery catch.`
- Shot 4: `Medium side profile, 35mm, lateral tracking. Redirects momentum behind back, nunchaku over shoulder into fast figure-eight.`
- Shot 5: `Low dynamic full-body, 24mm, slight arc around performer. Deep low stance, sweeps nunchaku across body level, rises sharply, spins once.`
- Shot 6: `Medium-close low angle, 40mm, camera settles. Final spin into controlled catch, lands in grounded martial stance, breathing steady.`

## 三、武侠/仙侠大片节奏（东方玄幻范例结构）

白衣女侠折扇横扫千军（15 秒 8 镜）示范了"爽片节拍"：
1. **天降神兵** Crash Landing / Speed-Ramp：大远景金光柱下坠，落地瞬间切超慢动作特写脚踩龟裂焦土，扬尘爆炸。
2. **起手式** Power Burst / Wide：全景固定，单腿旋转，折扇扫出金色环形冲击波。
3. **横扫千军** Tracking + Motion Blur：中景转全景跟拍，人物在敌阵中穿梭，击飞瞬间局部慢动作。
4. **分身压制** Bullet-time / Low-angle Push-in：低角度极慢推近，身后多个同步白衣残影，杂兵定格悬浮，空间扭曲。
5. **法相觉醒** Epic Scale / Slow Tilt-up：大远景上摇，背后拔地而起半透明发光神女法相，凝化巨剑，色调由金灰转冷白幽蓝。
6. **火龙吞噬** Hollywood VFX / Handheld Shake：远景，镜头随爆炸剧烈震动，地裂喷火龙，蘑菇云。
7. **废墟漫步** Slow Push-in：大远景转中全景平稳推镜，与前段激战形成反差，火星灰烬飘落。
8. **清冷回眸** Near Shot / Subtle Breathing：中近景固定，直视镜头，冷灰蓝空灵收尾。

## 四、功夫长镜头（MkAnime）的镜头/动作哲学

- **镜头**：`Professional long-take choreography with invisible cuts. Mostly 35–40mm lens. Smooth Steadicam tracking, lateral movement, controlled orbits and brief low-angle inserts. Keep screen direction.`
- **双方风格对比写法**：一方 `large committed attacks, expression from confidence to frustration to fierce concentration`；另一方 `calm economy, avoids by centimeters, tiny shifts, circular footwork, wrist control, hip rotation and redirection rather than brute force, composed expression`。
- 这种"力量型 vs 技巧型"对比是动作 prompt 里制造张力的关键。

## 五、MMA/格斗体育蒙太奇（6 镜结构）

1. `Extremely low ground-level tracking shot. Already moving at full speed across octagon toward heavy bag, violent combination ending in takedown slam, barely resets breathing.`
2. `Whip pan transition into impossibly fast striking combination, punches and kicks blur, camera struggles to keep up.`
3. `Wide moving shot across entire training facility, rapid technique changes back-to-back, cameraman nearly loses balance backwards through cage door.`
4. `Compressed long-lens shot, massive spinning heel kick in slow motion, sweat exploding off body, nearly overextends before recovering.`
5. `Ultra-low circular tracking around extended ground control, high-speed positional changes, calm breathing mid-roll.`
6. `Final devastating finishing combination, stands completely still over bag, sweat and chalk dust hanging in air, locks into still final pose under harsh cage lighting, sound cuts instantly, freeze frame, fade.`

## 六、动作戏镜头术语速查

- **速度层级**：`real-time` / `slow motion` / `super slow-mo` / `speed ramp` / `bullet time`
- **冲击强化**：`impact frame`（命中帧）、`whiplash motion`、`sweat spray`、`debris flying`、`dust explosion on landing`、`weight and momentum on every hit`
- **跟随方式**：`steadicam follow`（平滑）/ `handheld`（粗粝）/ `locked-off`（机位死稳主体动）/ `whip pan tracking`（急摇追打）
- **机位偏好**：动作全景用 `low angle`（显高大）、快攻用 `whip pan + fast push-in`、收尾用 `slow push-in` 或 `freeze frame`
- **转场掩蔽**：`transitions masked by body motion and impacts`（用身体/撞击挡镜切）
- **屏幕方向**：`keep screen direction consistent`（打斗中保持 180° 轴线，否则观众晕）

## 七、常见错误

- 写 `epic fight scene` 而不写身体力学 → 模型给你两个人摆 pose。
- 忘了写"weight"（重量感）→ 拳头像打在空气里。
- 动作太密又要求慢动作 →  readable choreography（可读的动作）丢失，必须交替 real-time / slow-mo。
- 加了 `glowing aura / magic particles` 又想要写实 → 风格冲突，negative prompt 里显式排除。
- 一镜到底不要写 `cut to`；多镜头才写 `SHOT 1 / SHOT 2`。
