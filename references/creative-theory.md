# Creative Theory Integration

The system integrates several compatible frameworks without forcing a single formula: cause-and-effect scene design, character desire/need arcs, three-act/five-act/hero-cycle structures, beat-based pacing, subtext/iceberg dialogue, show-don’t-tell actionability, scene objective/obstacle/turn, visual motif, montage, continuity editing, and genre convention.

Conflict resolution: theory is selected based on the project. A thriller may prioritize information asymmetry; a romance may prioritize proximity and subtext; horror may prioritize anticipation and familiarity distortion; action may prioritize spatial readability and action matching.

The engine does not imitate living artists or copy identifiable contemporary works; it extracts high-level formal properties such as symmetry, long-lens compression, color restraint, temporal fragmentation, or blocking style.
